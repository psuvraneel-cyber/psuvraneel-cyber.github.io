SnapSpec is not a government app. It is an independent tool made by Suvraneel Paul and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or acting on behalf of any government department or agency, examination body, university, bank or portal.

What SnapSpec does and does not do

  • It resizes and compresses your photos, signatures and PDFs on your phone to match published upload rules.
  • It does not submit applications, connect to any official portal, or provide any government service.
  • It does not guarantee that a file will be accepted.

Where the requirements come from

The photo, signature and document size and format requirements shown in the app are collected from publicly available official notifications and portal instructions. They can change at any time, sometimes for each exam cycle. Always confirm the current requirements on the official website before you apply.
