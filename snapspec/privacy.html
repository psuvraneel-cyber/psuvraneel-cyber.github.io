SnapSpec: Photo & PDF Tools ("SnapSpec", "the app") is developed by Suvraneel Paul ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. SnapSpec has no user accounts and we do not run any servers that receive your documents.

1. Your photos, signatures and documents

Every photo, signature, scan or PDF you choose in SnapSpec is processed on your phone. We never upload, receive, store or see these files.

  • Vault: fitted files you save are kept only in the app's private storage on your device, together with basic details such as the portal preset, file size and date. You can delete them at any time from the Vault. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • Name strip: a candidate name and date you type are drawn onto the image on your device and are not sent anywhere.
  • Saving and sharing: files leave the app only when you tap Save (to your Gallery or a folder you choose) or Share (to an app you pick). That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Settings: your display settings (theme and high contrast) are stored on your device.
  • On-device features: document scanning, text recognition (OCR), face guides for passport photos and background removal use Google's ML Kit, which processes your images on your phone. Your images are not sent to us or to Google for these features. ML Kit and Google Play services may send Google anonymous usage and diagnostic statistics (no images); see Google's ML Kit terms.
  • Passwords: a password you type to unlock a PDF is used on your phone only and is not stored.
  • Temporary copies: files you share into SnapSpec from another app are copied to the app's cache only long enough to read them and are then deleted. Copies made for the Share button are deleted the next time you share or open the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

SnapSpec is free because it shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). This is why the app requires an internet connection. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (for example ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Google uses this data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ads may be personalised based on this information where permitted. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (wording varies by device).

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • SnapSpec does not connect to, or submit anything to, any government or exam portal.

5. Children

SnapSpec is intended for adults and students preparing applications. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. Files in the Vault stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for ads is handled under Google's policies; you can manage it with the controls in section 2.

7. Security

Because your documents are processed and stored only on your device, they are protected by your phone's own security (screen lock and app sandboxing). Please keep your phone locked and up to date.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. Since we do not hold your personal data, most requests are handled by deleting files in the app or using Google's ad controls. You can always contact us with questions.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features. The effective date at the top will change, and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
