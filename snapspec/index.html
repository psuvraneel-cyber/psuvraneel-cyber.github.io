Private PDF & photo tools. Every SnapSpec tool works on your phone, and nothing you open is uploaded. No accounts, no cloud.

  • Scan documents, fill & sign PDFs, and extract text (OCR).
  • Merge, split, organise, compress, redact, watermark and unlock PDFs. Text stays selectable where pages aren't edited.
  • Passport & visa photos (US, UK, Canada, Australia, India e-Visa) sized to the official rules, without editing your face or background.
  • Convert HEIC to JPG, resize images, fit files under 100 KB, 1 MB or any limit.
  • Presets for exam and ID portals with strict photo, signature and PDF rules, with a check of the finished file.
SnapSpec is an independent app and is not affiliated with any government body, exam board, bank or portal. Always confirm requirements on the official website.
