SnapSpec: Photo & PDF Tools

Fit photos, signatures and PDFs to exam and ID portal rules

SnapSpec resizes, crops and compresses your photo, signature and documents to the exact pixel size, file size (KB) and format that exam, banking and ID portals ask for. Pick your portal, add your file, and save a file that fits.

  • Processed on your phone: your documents are never uploaded.
  • Checks the finished file's real pixel size, KB and DPI.
  • Builds multi-page PDFs from photos or scans.
SnapSpec is an independent app and is not affiliated with any government body, exam board, bank or portal. Always confirm requirements on the official website.
